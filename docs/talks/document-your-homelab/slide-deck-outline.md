# Slide Deck — How To Plan Your Perfect Homelab
20-minute talk (12 min slides, 5 min live demo, 3 min Q&A). Streamlined 8-slide deck.

## Slide 1: Title
- Title: How To Plan Your Perfect Homelab
- Subtitle: A 20-minute iterative planning method
- Presenter info & repo links

## Slide 2: The Accretion Problem
- One-liner: "A homelab grows one impulse-buy at a time — and the lab you can't explain is the lab you can't recover."
- Visual: Diagram showing messy accretion vs. structured planning enabling recovery & right-sizing.

## Slide 3: The Iterative Planning Loop
- Visual: 4-phase loop diagram (Goal → Achievable → How to → Fit → Goal).
- Key takeaway: Documentation is a byproduct of iterative planning. Enter anywhere (greenfield at Goal, existing lab at How-to or Fit).
- Method principle: Achievable precedes How-to to filter unviable ideas before writing design specs.

## Slide 4: AI as a Structured Interviewer
- Core concept: AI assistant is an interviewer forcing you to confront gaps, not a ghostwriter.
- Brief overview of the 4 session prompts driving the loop.

## Slide 5: Live Demo — Walking the Loop
- Transition slide for live walk-through (`/docs/homelab` example).
- Show Ideal State map (Goal), deferred ADRs (Achievable), environment docs (How-to), and the side-by-side gap matrix (Fit).

## Slide 6: The Take-Home — `homelab-doc-kit`
- Portable, zero-dependency scaffolding (`bootstrap.sh`).
- Includes phase templates, relative-linked markdown, and AI session prompts.

## Slide 7: Call to Action — Start with Goal
- Immediate step: start with Goal on paper or with an AI today, even if your "lab" is just a wishlist.

## Slide 8: Q&A & Resources
- Link to repo, `homelab-doc-kit`, and contact info.
